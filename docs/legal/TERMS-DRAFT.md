# Terms of use: draft clauses for legal review

**Status: DRAFT. Not published. Not legal advice.** A lawyer reviews these before any of them is
added to the live Terms (`legal/terms` in the database). The site has no accounts or sign-up, so
these clauses govern use of the website, its decks and its data, not a product subscription.

## Acceptable use

You may read, share links to, and download the investor decks for evaluating WonderApps and its
products. You must not, and must not help or allow anyone else to:

1. copy, scrape, crawl, harvest or extract content or data from the site by automated means
   (bots, scripts, crawlers, AI agents or browsing agents), except search engines indexing public
   pages in line with `robots.txt`;
2. use any content of the site, including text, decks, screenshots, figures and tracker data, to
   train, fine-tune, evaluate or ground a machine-learning or AI model, or to build a dataset;
3. reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any part of the site, except to the extent the
   applicable law expressly permits despite this restriction;
4. use the site or its content to build, design or benchmark a competing product or service;
5. circumvent rate limits, access controls, `robots.txt` directives or other technical measures;
6. access any non-public area (including the founder dashboard) or attempt to.

No licence is granted except the limited right to view the site described above.
